A garden gives us a gentle reminder that care takes time. This sample volunteer day imagines neighbours preparing a small courtyard garden and creating a space to enjoy together.

## Make something grow

Participants could help prepare pots, add compost, plant suitable seasonal seedlings, and label garden beds. Tasks would be matched to people’s comfort and mobility, with seated work available.

## Shared care after the day

Planting is only the beginning. An example watering rota and simple maintenance plan would help volunteers continue looking after the space without depending on any one person.

## More than a garden

The real invitation is to spend time together. A courtyard can become a place for conversation, learning, and a little everyday calm. This story illustrates a possible activity rather than documenting a completed event.
