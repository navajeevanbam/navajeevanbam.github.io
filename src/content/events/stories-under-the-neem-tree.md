A book can be an invitation to a much bigger world. Our sample reading afternoon makes space for curious questions, new words, and the confidence that grows when someone takes time to listen.

## Read, imagine, create

We picture small groups sharing age-appropriate stories with adult facilitators. After reading, participants can draw a character, invent an ending, or talk about a favourite moment. Activities are designed to be welcoming to different learning styles.

## How volunteers can contribute

Volunteers would support the session under the guidance of the program team. You can help arrange books, prepare art materials, or practise reading aloud. Any future work with children will follow the Ashram’s confirmed safeguarding and supervision arrangements.

## Every voice matters

There is no race to finish a book. The aim is to help each learner feel comfortable asking questions and sharing ideas.
