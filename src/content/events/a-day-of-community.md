Community is built in the moments we choose to share. This example open day brings together the spirit of our programs: learning, nourishment, companionship, and the simple experience of belonging.

## Meet the people behind the idea

An open day is an opportunity to ask questions, learn how volunteering might work, and share what your own neighbourhood needs. We imagine friendly conversation rather than a formal presentation.

## A place to participate

Visitors could join a reading corner, help arrange a shared table, or contribute ideas for future activities. Participation should be voluntary, accessible, and comfortable.
