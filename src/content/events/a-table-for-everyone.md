Some of the best conversations happen over a simple meal. This example community kitchen day invites neighbours and volunteers to prepare seasonal food, set a welcoming table, and enjoy an afternoon together.

## A little help goes a long way

You do not need to be an experienced cook. There is a role for people who enjoy organising, greeting guests, arranging seating, or keeping the space tidy. A coordinator would explain safe kitchen practices and match each volunteer with a suitable task.

## What to bring

Wear comfortable clothing and closed shoes. Bring a reusable water bottle and a willingness to work alongside others. Please discuss accessibility needs or dietary requirements with the team before any confirmed visit.

## A gathering with room for everyone

The most valuable part of this day is the welcome we create. We imagine a table where every guest can eat at their own pace and feel included in conversation.
