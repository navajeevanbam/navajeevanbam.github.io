Being present is a meaningful way to give. This illustrative gathering is about spending unhurried time with older neighbours: hearing their stories, sharing familiar songs, and enjoying an ordinary afternoon together.

## Come ready to listen

Conversation should follow the interests and comfort of each person. Some guests may love a lively game, while others may prefer a quiet cup of tea. There is space for both.

## Simple moments of connection

Our example program includes conversation tables, music chosen by participants, and familiar board games. Comfortable seating and opportunities to take a break would be part of the plan.

## Thoughtful participation

Please contact the team before attending any future gathering. Visits must be arranged and confirmed, and personal stories or photographs should never be shared without permission.
