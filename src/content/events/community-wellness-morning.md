Access to care begins with feeling able to ask a question. This example past event describes the kind of welcoming health-awareness morning we would like to offer with qualified local partners.

## A comfortable place to ask

A community setting can help people voice concerns they have been putting off. A future confirmed event would make space for private conversations and clear information about appropriate local care.

## Connecting people with support

The role of an outreach gathering is to make reliable services easier to find. Any clinical checks would be delivered only by suitably qualified professionals with informed consent.
