Ahalya Nivas is the Ashram’s home for older women who lack the support needed for food, healthcare, and a place to stay. It serves women who are unable to earn a livelihood and need domestic and social support.

## A home and a community

The service brings shelter and everyday care together. The accompanying photograph shows the Ashram’s Annalaxmi community programme, rather than the Ahalya Nivas residence.
