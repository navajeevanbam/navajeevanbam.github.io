The Ashram maintains a Child Care Home for children who need care and support, including orphaned children. Its work includes accommodation, food, education, and other needs as they arise.

## A place to grow

Daily care and learning sit alongside one another, giving children a supportive environment. Contact the Ashram to learn about ways to help this work.
