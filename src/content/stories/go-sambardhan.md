Navajeevan Goshala cares for cows and calves, including stray cattle and animals donated by supporters. Feeding and healthcare are central to its work.

## Care for animals

This work follows the guidelines of Bharatiya Gobansha Rakhyana Parisada. Contact the Ashram for current information about supporting the Goshala.
